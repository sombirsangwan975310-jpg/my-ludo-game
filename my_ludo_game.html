📜 Ludo rules

  1. Players: 2, 3, or 4 players. Each player has four tokens.
  2. Start: Roll a 6 to move a token out of your yard.
  3. Move: Roll the dice and move one token forward by that number.
  4. Extra turn: Rolling a 6 gives another roll. Capturing an opponent also gives an extra turn.
  5. Capture: Landing on an opponent on an unprotected square sends that token home.
  6. Safe squares: Star squares protect tokens from capture.
  7. Finish: You need an exact roll to enter the finish. First player to finish all four tokens wins.

This is a playable simplified Ludo implementation. Rule variations can differ between Ludo apps and house rules.

How to play

Roll the dice, then click one of the glowing tokens to move it. If you roll a 6, you may bring a token out of the yard or move a token already on the track.

Robot turns happen automatically. A token on a star square cannot be captured.
